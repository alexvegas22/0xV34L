* Why Hugo?

As a "DevOps" person, I'm usually surrounded by go applications, and as an Emacs
enthusiast, the org file support made the choice to use Hugo pretty easy.

I've used Hugo many times in the past, to make a nd update sites for multiple student clubs/associations, including my web-hosting student club.
